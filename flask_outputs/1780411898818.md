CONSERVATION OF MASS IN STEADY-STATE NON-REACTING SYSTEMS WITH MULTIPLE INLETS AND OUTLETS

Problem 1
Two cylindrical venous capillary vessels join to form a larger cylindrical venule to return blood to the heart. The first vessel has a diameter of $0.0006 \text{ cm}$, and an average blood flow velocity of $0.07 \text{ cm/s}$. The second vessel has a diameter of $0.0007 \text{ cm}$ and an average blood flow velocity of $0.08 \text{ cm/s}$. The diameter of the collecting vessel is $0.0008 \text{ cm.}$

Find the average blood flow velocity and the mass flow rate of blood in the collecting vessel. Assume blood density is unchanged.
($\rho_{\text{blood}} = 1.056 \frac{\text{g}}{\text{cm}^3}$)
(Draw:)

[IMAGE: Control volume diagram of the capillary junction. A box with solid lines and a dashed outer boundary representing the system boundary. There are two inlets from the left: inlet 1 (top-left, with an arrow pointing down-right into the box, labeled with a circled $\dot{M}_1$) and inlet 2 (bottom-left, with an arrow pointing right into the box, labeled with a circled $\dot{M}_2$). There is one outlet on the right (with an arrow pointing right out of the box, labeled with a circled $\dot{M}_3$).]

ASSUMP!
1. density = unchanged
2. steady-state
3. open-system
4. no rxns
5. S.S.

$\dot{m}_1 \Rightarrow \begin{aligned}[t] d_1 &= 6 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm} \\ v_1 &= 0.07 \text{ cm/s} \end{aligned}$

$\dot{m}_2 \Rightarrow \begin{aligned}[t] d_2 &= 7 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm} \\ v_2 &= 0.08 \text{ cm/s} \end{aligned}$

$\dot{m}_3 \Rightarrow \begin{aligned}[t] d &= 8 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm} \end{aligned}$

Step ①:
$$ \boxed{\dot{m}_1 + \dot{m}_2 = \dot{m}_3} $$
$$ A_1 v_1 + A_2 v_2 = A_3 v_3 $$
$$ \pi r_1^2 v_1 + \pi r_2^2 v_2 = \pi r_3^2 v_3 $$
$$ \pi \left(\frac{6 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 (0.07 \text{ cm/s}) + \pi \left(\frac{7 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 (0.08 \text{ cm/s}) = \pi \left(\frac{8 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 v_3 $$
$$ 1.979 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} + 3.077 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = 5.027 \times 10^{-7} \text{ cm}^2 v_3 $$
$$ 5.056 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = 5.027 \times 10^{-7} \text{ cm}^2 (v_3) $$
$$ \boxed{v_3 = 0.101 \text{ cm/s}} $$

Step ②:
$$ \dot{m}_1 + \dot{m}_2 = \dot{m}_3 $$
$$ \cancel{\rho}_1 \dot{V}_1 + \cancel{\rho}_2 \dot{V}_2 = \cancel{\rho}_3 \dot{V}_3 $$
$$ \rho \dot{V}_1 + \rho \dot{V}_2 = \rho \dot{V}_3 $$
$$ 1.056 \frac{\text{g}}{\text{cm}^3} (1.979 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s}) + 1.056 \frac{\text{g}}{\text{cm}^3} (3.077 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s}) = 1.056 \frac{\text{g}}{\text{cm}^3} (5.056 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s}) $$
$$ 2.089 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} + 3.249 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} = 5.338 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} $$
$$ \boxed{5.338 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} = 5.338 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s}} $$

# 📌 Global Key Concepts
-   **Conservation of Mass:** The total mass of blood entering a system must equal the total mass of blood leaving a system for steady-state, non-reacting, open systems.
-   **Steady-State System:** System properties do not change with time.
-   **Non-Reacting System:** No chemical reactions occur within the system that would change the mass of the components.
-   **Open System:** Mass can cross the system boundaries (inlets and outlets).
-   **Mass Flow Rate ($\dot{m}$):** The mass of substance flowing per unit time, calculated as $\dot{m} = \rho \dot{V} = \rho A v$, where $\rho$ is density, $A$ is cross-sectional area, and $v$ is average velocity.
-   **Volumetric Flow Rate ($\dot{V}$):** The volume of fluid flowing per unit time, calculated as $\dot{V} = A v$.
-   **Incompressible Fluid:** A fluid whose density remains constant, often a reasonable assumption for blood in many biological contexts.

# 📐 Global Key Equations
-   **General Mass Balance for Steady-State, Non-Reacting, Open Systems:**
    $$ \sum \dot{m}_{\text{in}} = \sum \dot{m}_{\text{out}} $$
    -   For problems with two inlets and one outlet:
    $$ \dot{m}_1 + \dot{m}_2 = \dot{m}_3 $$
-   **Mass Flow Rate:**
    $$ \dot{m} = \rho \dot{V} = \rho A v $$
-   **Volumetric Flow Rate:**
    $$ \dot{V} = A v $$
-   **Area of a Circular Cross-Section:**
    $$ A = \pi r^2 = \pi \left(\frac{d}{2}\right)^2 $$
