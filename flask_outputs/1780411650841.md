**CONSERVATION OF MASS IN STEADY-STATE NON-REACTING SYSTEMS WITH MULTIPLE INLETS AND OUTLETS**

**Problem 1**
Two cylindrical venous capillary vessels join to form a larger cylindrical venule to return blood to the heart. The first vessel has a diameter of 0.0006 cm, and an average blood flow velocity of 0.07 cm/s. The second vessel has a diameter of 0.0007 cm and an average blood flow velocity of 0.0007 cm 0.08 cm/s. The diameter of the collecting vessel is 0.0008 cm.

$$\rho_{\text{blood}} = 1.056 \frac{\text{g}}{\text{cm}^3}$$

Find the average blood flow velocity and the mass flow rate of blood in the collecting vessel. Assume blood density is unchanged.

Draw:
[IMAGE: A schematic block diagram showing a system with two inlets and one outlet. The control volume is outlined by a dashed black rectangle. An inlet arrow enters from the top labeled $\dot{M}_1$. Another inlet arrow enters from the left labeled $\dot{M}_2$. An outlet arrow exits to the right labeled $\dot{M}_3$.]

**ASSUMPT**
*   ↳ density = unchanged
*   ↳ steady-state
*   ↳ open-system
*   ↳ no rxns
*   ↳ STP

**Givens:**
$$\dot{M}_1 \Rightarrow d_1 = 6 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}$$
$$v_1 = 0.07 \text{ cm/s}$$

$$\dot{M}_2 \Rightarrow d_2 = 7 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}$$
$$v_2 = 0.08 \text{ cm/s}$$

$$\dot{M}_3 \Rightarrow d = 8 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}$$

**Step ①:**
$$\dot{m}_1 + \dot{m}_2 = \dot{m}_3$$

$$A_1 v_1 + A_2 v_2 = A_3 v_3$$
$$\pi r_1^2 v_1 + \pi r_2^2 v_2 = \pi r_3^2 v_3$$

$$\pi \left(\frac{6 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 (0.07 \text{ cm/s}) + \pi \left(\frac{7 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 (0.08 \text{ cm/s}) = \pi \left(\frac{8 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 v_3$$

$$1.979 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} + 3.077 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = 5.027 \times 10^{-7} \text{ cm}^2 v_3$$

$$5.056 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = 5.027 \times 10^{-7} \text{ cm}^2 (v_3)$$

$$v_3 = 0.101 \text{ cm/s}$$

**Step ②:**
$$\dot{m}_1 + \dot{m}_2 = \dot{m}_3$$
$$\rho \dot{V}_1 + \rho \dot{V}_2 = \rho \dot{V}_3$$
$$\rho v_1 A_1 + \rho v_2 A_2 = \rho v_3 A_3$$

$$1.056 \text{ g/cm}^3 \left(1.979 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s}\right) + 1.056 \text{ g/cm}^3 \left(3.077 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s}\right) = 1.056 \text{ g/cm}^3 \left(5.056 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s}\right)$$

$$2.089 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} + 3.249 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} = 5.338 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s}$$
$$\times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s}$$

$$5.338 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} = 5.338 \times 10^{-8} \frac{\text{g}}{\text{s}}$$

# 📌 Global Key Concepts
-   **Conservation of Mass:** For steady-state, non-reacting systems, the total mass flow rate into a control volume must equal the total mass flow rate out of the control volume.
-   **Steady-State System:** System properties do not change with time.
-   **Open System:** Mass and energy can cross the system boundaries.
-   **Mass Flow Rate ($\dot{m}$):** The mass of substance flowing per unit time. Can be expressed as $\rho \dot{V}$ or $\rho A v$.
-   **Volumetric Flow Rate ($\dot{V}$):** The volume of substance flowing per unit time. Can be expressed as $A v$.
-   **Biomedical Applications:** Principles of mass conservation are critical for analyzing blood flow in the cardiovascular system, including disease states like coronary ischemia and surgical interventions like bypass grafting.
-   **Plaque Formation:** Reduces vessel diameter and blood flow velocity, leading to reduced oxygen supply (ischemia).
-   **Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG):** Surgical procedure to redirect blood flow around obstructed coronary arteries using grafts.

# 📐 Global Key Equations
-   **Conservation of Mass (Steady-State, Non-Reacting, Open System):**
    $$ \sum_{\text{in}} \dot{m} = \sum_{\text{out}} \dot{m} $$
-   **Mass Flow Rate:**
    $$ \dot{m} = \rho \dot{V} $$
    $$ \dot{m} = \rho A v $$
    where $\rho$ is density, $\dot{V}$ is volumetric flow rate, $A$ is cross-sectional area, and $v$ is average velocity.
-   **Volumetric Flow Rate:**
    $$ \dot{V} = A v $$
-   **Area of a Cylinder/Circle:**
    $$ A = \pi r^2 = \pi \left(\frac{d}{2}\right)^2 $$
    where $r$ is radius and $d$ is diameter.
