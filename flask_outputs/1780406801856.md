# 📝 Problems & Solutions
**Problem Set 3.2**

**CONSERVATION OF MASS IN STEADY-STATE NON-REACTING SYSTEMS WITH MULTIPLE INLETS AND OUTLETS**

**Problem 1**
Two cylindrical venous capillary vessels join to form a larger cylindrical venule to return blood to the heart. The first vessel has a diameter of 0.0006 cm, and an average blood flow velocity of 0.07 cm/s. The second vessel has a diameter of 0.0007 cm and an average blood flow velocity of 0.08 cm/s. The diameter of the collecting vessel is 0.0008 cm.
$$\rho_{\text{blood}} = 1.056 \text{ g/cm}^3$$
Find the average blood flow velocity and the mass flow rate of blood in the collecting vessel. Assume blood density is unchanged.

**ASSUMPT** (Assumptions)
-   $$\text{density} = \text{unchanged}$$
-   $$\text{Steady-state}$$
-   $$\text{Open-system}$$
-   $$\text{No rxns}$$
-   $$\text{STP}$$

**Inlet 1:**
-   $$\dot{m}_1 = ? \Rightarrow d_1 = 6 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}$$
-   $$v_1 = 0.07 \text{ cm/s}$$

**Inlet 2:**
-   $$\dot{m}_2 = ? \Rightarrow d_2 = 7 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}$$
-   $$v_2 = 0.08 \text{ cm/s}$$

**Outlet 3:**
-   $$\dot{m}_3 = ? \Rightarrow d_3 = 8 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}$$

Step ①:
-   $$\boxed{\dot{m}_1 + \dot{m}_2 = \dot{m}_3}$$
-   $$A_1 v_1 + A_2 v_2 = A_3 v_3$$
-   $$\pi r_1^2 v_1 + \pi r_2^2 v_2 = \pi r_3^2 v_3$$
-   $$\pi \left( \frac{6 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2} \right)^2 (0.07 \text{ cm/s}) + \pi \left( \frac{7 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2} \right)^2 (0.08 \text{ cm/s}) = \pi \left( \frac{8 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2} \right)^2 v_3$$
-   $$1.979 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} + 3.077 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = 5.027 \times 10^{-7} \text{ cm}^2 \cdot v_3$$
-   $$5.056 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = 5.027 \times 10^{-7} \text{ cm}^2 (v_3)$$
-   $$\boxed{v_3 = 0.101 \text{ cm/s}}$$

Step ②:
-   $$\dot{m}_1 + \dot{m}_2 = \dot{m}_3$$
-   $$\rho \dot{V}_1 + \rho \dot{V}_2 = \rho \dot{V}_3$$
-   $$\rho v_1 A_1 + \rho v_2 A_2 = \rho v_3 A_3$$
-   $$1.056 \text{ g/cm}^3 \left( 1.979 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} \right) + 1.056 \text{ g/cm}^3 \left( 3.077 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} \right) = 1.056 \text{ g/cm}^3 \left( 5.056 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3\text{/s} \right)$$
-   $$2.089 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} + 3.249 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} = 5.338 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s}$$
-   $$\boxed{5.338 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} = 5.338 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s}}$$
-   $$_{\times 10^{-8}}$$

# 📌 Key Concepts
-   **Conservation of Mass:** For a steady-state, non-reacting open system, the total mass flow rate into the system equals the total mass flow rate out of the system.
-   **Steady-State:** System properties do not change with time.
-   **Non-Reacting System:** No chemical reactions occur that would change the mass of components.
-   **Mass Flow Rate ($\dot{m}$):** The mass of a substance flowing per unit time.
-   **Volumetric Flow Rate ($\dot{V}$):** The volume of a substance flowing per unit time.
-   **Relationship between Mass and Volumetric Flow Rate:** $\dot{m} = \rho \dot{V}$, where $\rho$ is density.
-   **Relationship between Volumetric Flow Rate, Area, and Velocity:** $\dot{V} = A v$, where $A$ is cross-sectional area and $v$ is average velocity.
-   **Cylindrical Vessel Cross-Sectional Area:** $A = \pi r^2 = \pi (d/2)^2$.

# 📐 Key Equations
-   **Conservation of Mass (Steady-State, Non-Reacting, Open System):**
    $$ \sum_{\text{in}} \dot{m} = \sum_{\text{out}} \dot{m} $$
-   **Mass Flow Rate:**
    $$ \dot{m} = \rho \dot{V} $$
-   **Volumetric Flow Rate:**
    $$ \dot{V} = A v $$
-   **Cross-sectional Area of a Cylinder:**
    $$ A = \pi r^2 = \pi \left( \frac{d}{2} \right)^2 $$
