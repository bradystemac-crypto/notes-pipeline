**Problem Set 3.2**

**CONSERVATION OF MASS IN STEADY-STATE NON-REACTING SYSTEMS WITH MULTIPLE INLETS AND OUTLETS**

**Problem 1**
Two cylindrical venous capillary vessels join to form a larger cylindrical venule to return blood to the heart. The first vessel has a diameter of 0.0006 cm, and an average blood flow velocity of 0.07 cm/s. The second vessel has a diameter of 0.0007 cm and an average blood flow velocity of 0.08 cm/s. The diameter of the collecting vessel is 0.0008 cm.

$\rho_{blood} = 1.056 \text{ g}/\text{cm}^3$

Find the average blood flow velocity and the mass flow rate of blood in the collecting vessel. Assume blood density is unchanged.

**Assumptions & Setup:**
Draw:
[IMAGE: A schematic drawing of a control volume with a dashed boundary. An arrow labeled with circled 1 (①) enters from the top, another arrow labeled with circled 2 (②) enters from the left, and an arrow labeled with circled 3 (③) exits to the right.]

ASSUMPT
1. density = unchanged
2. steady-state
3. open-system
4. no rxns
5. STP

**Inlet/Outlet Parameters:**
$\dot{m}_1 \implies d_1 = 6 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}$
$\phantom{\dot{m}_1 \implies } v_1 = 0.07 \text{ cm/s}$

$\dot{m}_2 = ? \quad d_2 = 7 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}$
$\phantom{\dot{m}_2 = ? \quad } v_2 = 0.08 \text{ cm/s}$

$\dot{m}_3 = \quad d = 8 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}$

**Step ①:**
- $\dot{m}_1 + \dot{m}_2 = \dot{m}_3$
- $A_1 v_1 + A_2 v_2 = A_3 v_3$
- $\pi r_1^2 v_1 + \pi r_2^2 v_2 = \pi r_3^2 v_3$
- $\pi \left(\frac{6 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 (0.07 \text{ cm/s}) + \pi \left(\frac{7 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 (0.08 \text{ cm/s}) = \pi \left(\frac{8 \times 10^{-4} \text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 v_3$
- $1.979 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3/\text{s} + 3.077 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3/\text{s} = 5.027 \times 10^{-7} \text{ cm}^2 v_3$
- $5.056 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3/\text{s} = 5.024 \times 10^{-7} \text{ cm}^2 (v_3)$
- $v_3 = 0.102 \text{ cm/s}$

**Step ②:**
- $\dot{m}_1 + \dot{m}_2 = \dot{m}_3$
- $\rho \dot{V}_1 + \rho \dot{V}_2 = \rho \dot{V}_3$
- $\rho \dot{v}_1 + \rho \dot{v}_2 = \rho \dot{v}_3$
- $1.056 \text{ g/cm}^3 \left(1.979 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3/\text{s}\right) + 1.056 \text{ g/cm}^3 \left(3.077 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3/\text{s}\right) = 1.056 \text{ g/cm}^3 \left(5.056 \times 10^{-8} \text{ cm}^3/\text{s}\right)$
- $2.089 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} + 3.249 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} = 5.338 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s}$
- $5.338 \times 10^{-8} \text{ g/s} = 5.338 \times 10^{-8} \frac{\text{g}}{\text{s}}$

# 📌 Global Key Concepts
- **Conservation of Mass:** For steady-state, non-reacting, open systems, the total mass flow rate into a control volume equals the total mass flow rate out.
- **Volumetric Flow Rate ($\dot{V}$):** The volume of fluid passing through a cross-sectional area per unit time, calculated as $A \cdot v$ (Area $\times$ Velocity).
- **Mass Flow Rate ($\dot{m}$):** The mass of fluid passing through a cross-sectional area per unit time, calculated as $\rho \cdot \dot{V}$ (Density $\times$ Volumetric Flow Rate).
- **Area of a Circular Vessel:** $A = \pi r^2 = \pi \left(\frac{D}{2}\right)^2$.
- **Biomedical Engineering Applications:** Modeling blood flow in the circulatory system, including in healthy vessels, constricted regions, and bypass grafts.

# 📐 Global Key Equations
- **Conservation of Mass (Steady-State, Non-Reacting):**
    $$ \sum_{in} \dot{m} = \sum_{out} \dot{m} $$
- **Mass Flow Rate:**
    $$ \dot{m} = \rho \dot{V} $$
- **Volumetric Flow Rate:**
    $$ \dot{V} = A v $$
- **Area of a Circle:**
    $$ A = \pi r^2 = \pi \left(\frac{D}{2}\right)^2 $$
