**CONSERVATION OF MASS IN STEADY-STATE NON-REACTING SYSTEMS WITH MULTIPLE INLETS AND OUTLETS**
**Problem 1**
Two cylindrical venous capillary vessels join to form a larger cylindrical venule to return blood to the heart. The first vessel has a diameter of $0.0006\text{ cm}$, and an average blood flow velocity of $0.07\text{ cm/s}$. The second vessel has a diameter of $0.0007\text{ cm}$ and an average blood flow velocity of $0.08\text{ cm/s}$. The diameter of the collecting vessel is $0.0008\text{ cm}$.
Find the average blood flow velocity and the mass flow rate of blood in the collecting vessel. Assume blood density is unchanged.
($\rho_{\text{blood}} = 1.056\text{ g/cm}^3$)

   - Stream 2 ($\dot{M}_2$) entering from the bottom-left with an arrow pointing right.
   - Stream 3 ($\dot{M}_3$) exiting to the right with an arrow pointing right.
 - Circled labels $\dot{M}_1$, $\dot{M}_2$, $\dot{M}_3$ near each arrow.]

ASSUMPT:
1.  density = unchanged
2.  steady-state
3.  open-system
4.  no rxns
5.  STP

**Given:**
-   $\dot{M}_1$:
    -   $d_1 = 6 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm}$
    -   $v_1 = 0.07\text{ cm/s}$
-   $\dot{M}_2$:
    -   $d_2 = 7 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm}$
    -   $v_2 = 0.08\text{ cm/s}$
-   $\dot{M}_3$:
    -   $d_3 = 8 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm}$

Step ①: **Calculate average blood flow velocity in the collecting vessel ($v_3$)**
$$ \dot{M}_1 + \dot{M}_2 = \dot{M}_3 $$
Assuming constant density, mass balance simplifies to volumetric flow rate balance:
$$ \rho \dot{V}_1 + \rho \dot{V}_2 = \rho \dot{V}_3 \implies \dot{V}_1 + \dot{V}_2 = \dot{V}_3 $$
Since $\dot{V} = A v$:
$$ A_1 v_1 + A_2 v_2 = A_3 v_3 $$
Using $A = \pi r^2 = \pi (d/2)^2$:
$$ \pi \left(\frac{d_1}{2}\right)^2 v_1 + \pi \left(\frac{d_2}{2}\right)^2 v_2 = \pi \left(\frac{d_3}{2}\right)^2 v_3 $$
$$ \pi \left(\frac{6 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 (0.07\text{ cm/s}) + \pi \left(\frac{7 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 (0.08\text{ cm/s}) = \pi \left(\frac{8 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 v_3 $$
Calculate individual volumetric flow rates:
-   $\dot{V}_1 = \pi (3 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm})^2 (0.07\text{ cm/s}) = \pi (9 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^2) (0.07\text{ cm/s}) = 1.979 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}$
-   $\dot{V}_2 = \pi (3.5 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm})^2 (0.08\text{ cm/s}) = \pi (1.225 \times 10^{-7}\text{ cm}^2) (0.08\text{ cm/s}) = 3.077 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}$
Substitute values into the equation:
$$ 1.979 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} + 3.077 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = \pi \left(\frac{8 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 v_3 $$
$$ 5.056 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = \pi (4 \times 10^{-4}\text{ cm})^2 v_3 $$
$$ 5.056 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = 5.027 \times 10^{-7}\text{ cm}^2 v_3 $$
$$ v_3 = \frac{5.056 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}}{5.027 \times 10^{-7}\text{ cm}^2} $$
$$ \boxed{v_3 = 0.101\text{ cm/s}} $$

Step ②: **Calculate mass flow rate of blood in the collecting vessel ($\dot{M}_3$)**
$$ \dot{M}_1 + \dot{M}_2 = \dot{M}_3 $$
Since $\dot{M} = \rho \dot{V}$:
$$ \rho_1 \dot{V}_1 + \rho_2 \dot{V}_2 = \rho_3 \dot{V}_3 $$
Given density is unchanged, $\rho_1 = \rho_2 = \rho_3 = \rho = 1.056\text{ g/cm}^3$:
$$ \rho (\dot{V}_1 + \dot{V}_2) = \rho \dot{V}_3 $$
We know $\dot{V}_1 = 1.979 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}$ and $\dot{V}_2 = 3.077 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}$.
So, $\dot{V}_3 = \dot{V}_1 + \dot{V}_2 = 5.056 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}$.
Now, calculate mass flow rates:
-   $\dot{M}_1 = 1.056\text{ g/cm}^3 (1.979 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}) = 2.089 \times 10^{-8}\text{ g/s}$
-   $\dot{M}_2 = 1.056\text{ g/cm}^3 (3.077 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}) = 3.249 \times 10^{-8}\text{ g/s}$
-   $\dot{M}_3 = 1.056\text{ g/cm}^3 (5.056 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}) = 5.338 \times 10^{-8}\text{ g/s}$
Verify mass balance:
$$ 2.089 \times 10^{-8}\text{ g/s} + 3.249 \times 10^{-8}\text{ g/s} = 5.338 \times 10^{-8}\text{ g/s} $$
$$ \boxed{\boxed{5.338 \times 10^{-8}\text{ g/s} = 5.338 \times 10^{-8}\text{ g/s}}} $$

**Problem 2**
Let's reconsider the coronary artery problem for Tuesday's class. Turns out our assumption of no blood accumulation in the plaque region was wrong and the patient has developed coronary ischemia (blood flow is obstructed at that location). Patients with coronary ischemia may undergo a surgical treatment called coronary artery bypass grafting, which uses a blood vessel graft to connect the segments of the vessel with normal blood flow to redirect blood around the blocked points. The patient's surgeon wants to implant a graft to bypass the constricted region (see figure) and reaches out to you as the cardiovascular engineer to design an appropriate synthetic graft.

Remember, the diameter of a healthy coronary artery is $2.5\text{ mm}$ and the diameter of the area with a plaque decreased by two-thirds for this patient. The blood flow rate in a healthy artery is $6.4\text{ cm/s}$ and the density of blood is $1.056\text{ g/cm}^3$. The plaque decreased the blood flow velocity in that section of the artery by $25\%$. Assume no reactions with blood and the curvature of the graft does not alter blood flow meaning blood flows at the same velocity through the graft as in the healthy artery. Your goal is to return the system to steady state.

a. Draw a diagram of the system (with input and output streams).
b. Write the accounting equation for tracking the mass of blood.
c. What should be the mass flow rate through the graft?
d. What must be the diameter of the graft to achieve your goal?

**Conversion:** $1\text{ mm} = 0.1\text{ cm}$

**Given/Calculated Parameters:**
-   Healthy coronary artery diameter $D_{\text{healthy}} = 2.5\text{ mm} = 0.25\text{ cm}$
-   Healthy blood flow velocity $v_{\text{healthy}} = 6.4\text{ cm/s}$
-   Blood density $\rho = 1.056\text{ g/cm}^3$
-   Plaque region diameter $D_{\text{plaque}}$: decreased by two-thirds means $D_{\text{plaque}} = D_{\text{healthy}} - \frac{2}{3}D_{\text{healthy}} = \frac{1}{3}D_{\text{healthy}}$
    -   $D_{\text{plaque}} = \frac{1}{3} \times 2.5\text{ mm} = 0.833\text{ mm} \approx 0.083\text{ cm}$
-   Plaque region velocity $v_{\text{plaque}}$: decreased by $25\%$ means $v_{\text{plaque}} = v_{\text{healthy}} - 0.25 v_{\text{healthy}} = 0.75 v_{\text{healthy}}$
    -   $v_{\text{plaque}} = 0.75 \times 6.4\text{ cm/s} = 4.8\text{ cm/s}$
-   Graft velocity $v_{\text{graft}} = v_{\text{healthy}} = 6.4\text{ cm/s}$ (assumption)

    -   $D_3 = D_{\text{healthy}} = 0.25\text{ cm}$
    -   $v_3 = v_{\text{healthy}} = 6.4\text{ cm/s}$

b. **Accounting equation for tracking the mass of blood:**
For a steady-state, non-reacting open system with constant density:
$$ \boxed{\dot{M}_1 + \dot{M}_2 = \dot{M}_3} $$
Or in terms of volumetric flow rate:
$$ \dot{V}_1 + \dot{V}_2 = \dot{V}_3 $$

c. **Mass flow rate through the graft ($\dot{M}_2$):**
We want to restore the healthy flow rate post-plaque, so $\dot{M}_3$ should be the mass flow rate of a healthy artery.
The mass balance equation is:
$$ \dot{M}_1 + \dot{M}_2 = \dot{M}_3 $$
Which can be written as:
$$ \rho A_1 v_1 + \rho A_2 v_2 = \rho A_3 v_3 $$
Since density is constant and non-zero, we can divide by $\rho$:
$$ A_1 v_1 + A_2 v_2 = A_3 v_3 $$
Using $A = \pi (D/2)^2$:
$$ \pi \left(\frac{D_1}{2}\right)^2 v_1 + \pi \left(\frac{D_2}{2}\right)^2 v_2 = \pi \left(\frac{D_3}{2}\right)^2 v_3 $$
The goal is to find $\dot{M}_2$. Let's first calculate the known volumetric flow rates:
-   $\dot{V}_1 = \pi \left(\frac{0.083\text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 (4.8\text{ cm/s}) = \pi (0.0415\text{ cm})^2 (4.8\text{ cm/s}) = 0.0259\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}$
-   $\dot{V}_3 = \pi \left(\frac{0.25\text{ cm}}{2}\right)^2 (6.4\text{ cm/s}) = \pi (0.125\text{ cm})^2 (6.4\text{ cm/s}) = 0.314\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}$

Now, using volumetric flow rate balance:
$$ \dot{V}_1 + \dot{V}_2 = \dot{V}_3 $$
$$ 0.0259\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} + \dot{V}_2 = 0.314\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} $$
$$ \dot{V}_2 = 0.314\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} - 0.0259\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = 0.2881\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} $$
To find the mass flow rate $\dot{M}_2$:
$$ \dot{M}_2 = \rho \dot{V}_2 $$
$$ \dot{M}_2 = 1.056\text{ g/cm}^3 \times 0.2881\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} $$
$$ \boxed{\dot{M}_2 = 0.304\text{ g/s}} $$

The terms in the handwritten annotation:
-   $\dot{M}_1 = \rho \dot{V}_1 = 1.056\text{ g/cm}^3 \times 0.0259\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = 0.02735\text{ g/s} \approx 0.0274\text{ g/s}$
-   $\dot{M}_3 = \rho \dot{V}_3 = 1.056\text{ g/cm}^3 \times 0.314\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = 0.3315\text{ g/s} \approx 0.331\text{ g/s}$
So, $0.0274\text{ g/s} + \dot{M}_2 = 0.331\text{ g/s}$.
$\dot{M}_2 = 0.331\text{ g/s} - 0.0274\text{ g/s} = 0.3036\text{ g/s} \approx 0.304\text{ g/s}$. This matches.

d. **Diameter of the graft ($D_2$):**
We know $\dot{M}_2 = 0.304\text{ g/s}$ and $v_2 = 6.4\text{ cm/s}$.
$$ \dot{M}_2 = \rho v_2 A_2 $$
$$ 0.304\text{ g/s} = 1.056\frac{\text{g}}{\text{cm}^3} \cdot (6.4\text{ cm/s}) \cdot A_2 $$
First, find the volumetric flow rate, $\dot{V}_2$:
$$ \dot{V}_2 = \frac{0.304\text{ g/s}}{1.056\text{ g/cm}^3} = 0.28787\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} \approx 0.288\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} $$
Now use $\dot{V}_2 = A_2 v_2$:
$$ 0.288\text{ cm}^3\text{/s} = A_2 \cdot 6.4\text{ cm/s} $$
$$ A_2 = \frac{0.288\text{ cm}^3\text{/s}}{6.4\text{ cm/s}} = 0.045\text{ cm}^2 $$
Since $A_2 = \pi (D_2/2)^2$:
$$ 0.045\text{ cm}^2 = \pi \left(\frac{D_2}{2}\right)^2 $$
$$ \frac{0.045\text{ cm}^2}{\pi} = \left(\frac{D_2}{2}\right)^2 $$
$$ 0.01432\text{ cm}^2 = \left(\frac{D_2}{2}\right)^2 $$
$$ \sqrt{0.01432\text{ cm}^2} = \frac{D_2}{2} $$
$$ 0.1197\text{ cm} = \frac{D_2}{2} $$
$$ D_2 = 2 \times 0.1197\text{ cm} $$
$$ \boxed{D_2 = 0.239\text{ cm}} $$

# 📋 Global Review

### 📌 Key Concepts
-   **Conservation of Mass**: In a steady-state, non-reacting open system, the total mass flow rate into the system equals the total mass flow rate out of the system ($\sum \dot{M}_{\text{in}} = \sum \dot{M}_{\text{out}}$).
-   **Mass Flow Rate ($\dot{M}$)**: Defined as the product of density ($\rho$), cross-sectional area ($A$), and average velocity ($v$) of the fluid: $\dot{M} = \rho A v$.
-   **Volumetric Flow Rate ($\dot{V}$)**: Defined as the product of cross-sectional area ($A$) and average velocity ($v$): $\dot{V} = A v$.
-   **Incompressibility Assumption**: For blood, density is often assumed constant, simplifying mass balance to a volumetric flow rate balance ($\sum \dot{V}_{\text{in}} = \sum \dot{V}_{\text{out}}$).
-   **Coronary Ischemia**: A condition where blood flow to the heart muscle is obstructed, often due to plaque accumulation, leading to reduced velocity and diameter in affected vessels.
-   **Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)**: A surgical procedure to restore normal blood flow by creating new pathways (grafts) around blocked sections of coronary arteries.
-   **Design Goal in Biomedical Engineering**: To restore physiological parameters (e.g., healthy blood flow velocity and mass flow rate) to normal levels using engineered solutions.

### 📐 Key Equations
-   **General Mass Balance (Steady-State, Non-Reacting)**:
    $$ \sum \dot{M}_{\text{in}} = \sum \dot{M}_{\text{out}} $$
-   **Mass Flow Rate**:
    $$ \dot{M} = \rho \dot{V} = \rho A v $$
-   **Volumetric Flow Rate**:
    $$ \dot{V} = A v $$
-   **Area of a Circular Vessel**:
    $$ A = \pi r^2 = \pi \left(\frac{D}{2}\right)^2 $$
-   **Volumetric Flow Rate Balance (Constant Density)**:
    $$ \sum (A v)_{\text{in}} = \sum (A v)_{\text{out}} $$
