# 📌 Key Concepts
- **Conservation of Energy in Open Systems:** The first law of thermodynamics applied to systems with mass flow, considering heat transfer, non-flow work, and enthalpy changes.
- **Specific Enthalpy:** A thermodynamic property representing the total energy of a flowing fluid, per unit mass.
- **Sensible Heat:** The heat transferred to cause a change in temperature without a phase change, calculated using heat capacity ($C_P$) and temperature difference.
- **Latent Heat of Vaporization:** The energy required to change a substance from a liquid to a gas at a constant temperature.
- **Steady State:** A condition where system properties do not change with time.
- **Mass Balance:** For a steady-state open system, the mass flow rate in equals the mass flow rate out.

# 📐 Key Equations
- **General Energy Balance for Open Systems:**
  $$-\Delta \dot{H} + \dot{Q} + \dot{W}_{nf} = 0$$
- **Simplified Energy Balance (Problem 1, assuming no non-flow work):**
  $$-\Delta \dot{H} + \dot{Q} = 0$$
- **Total Enthalpy Change (Hypothetical Path):**
  $$\Delta \hat{H}_T = \Delta\hat{H}_{sens} + \Delta\hat{H}_{vap}$$
- **Specific Enthalpy Change for Sensible Heating (Liquid):**
  $$\Delta\hat{H} = C_p\Delta T$$
- **Specific Enthalpy Change for Sensible Heating (Air, general form):**
  $$\Delta \hat{H}_{sens} = \int_{T_1}^{T_2} C_P(T) dT$$
- **Specific Enthalpy of Vaporization:**
  $$\Delta \hat{H}_{vap} = 2,414.2 \text{ J/g} \text{ (at } 37^\circ C \text{ for water)}$$
- **Steady State Mass Balance for Open Systems:**
  $$\boxed{\dot{M}_{in} = \dot{M}_{out}}$$
